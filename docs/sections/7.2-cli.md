## Interfaces

```text
speakerops reports readiness --json
speakerops design publish --event E
speakerops schedule place --event E --session S --room R --start … --end …
```

Env **names:** `SPEAKEROPS_API_KEY`, `SPEAKEROPS_API_URL` (see `docs/SECRETS.md` / `docs/CLI.md`).

## Security

- Scopes enforced on Worker; CLI cannot self-elevate (E8/E10).
- High-risk default-deny: `comms:send`, `decisions:write`, `keys:admin`.
- No secrets in repo; env names only.
- Audit on writes with `correlationId` (key actor when Bearer).
